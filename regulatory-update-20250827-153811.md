## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **VAT Rate Reduction:** From July 1, 2023, to December 31, 2023, the VAT rate is reduced by 2%, applicable to goods and services currently subject to a 10% VAT rate [Ref 1]. This reduction does not apply to specific sectors [Ref 1].
- **Excluded Sectors:** The 2% VAT reduction does not apply to telecommunications, financial activities, banking, securities, insurance, real estate business, metals, pre-fabricated metal products, mining products (excluding coal), coke, refined petroleum, and chemical products [Ref 1]. Goods and services subject to special consumption tax are also excluded [Ref 1].
- **Invoice Requirements:** Businesses must declare the reduced VAT rate (8%) on their VAT invoices and are required to issue VAT invoices according to regulations [Ref 1].
- **Monthly VAT Returns:** Businesses are required to submit monthly VAT returns [Ref 2].
- **Late Payment Interest:** Interest is charged on late VAT payments [Ref 2]. The interest rate is determined by regulations [Ref 2].
