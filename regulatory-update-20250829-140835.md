## Tax & VAT Compliance
- The Danish National Tax Court clarified the VAT treatment for imported vehicles under the Danish VAT Act [Ref 1].
- Acquisition VAT is imposed on electric vehicles imported from Germany if they qualify as a new means of transport under the VAT Act [Ref 1].
- A vehicle is considered new if it has traveled less than 6,000 km or has been in use for less than six months [Ref 1].
- The right of disposal transferred to the buyer upon importation supports the assessment of VAT [Ref 1].
- The court upheld the tax authority’s interpretation that the conditions for exemption were not met and the VAT charge was valid [Ref 1].

## Implementation Impact
- Buyers of vehicles within the EU should note the distance driven and duration of use before import to avoid unexpected VAT liabilities [Ref 1].
