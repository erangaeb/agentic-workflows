## Topic
- regulatory updates

## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction/scope: Poland, Germany, South Africa, China, Vanuatu, Botswana. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6]

## Requirements / Specifications
- **Germany:** Structured XML formats are mandated for B2B e-invoicing. [Ref 2]

## Validation / Business Rules
- **Germany:** Human-readable copies of e-invoices are required only if they contain additional tax-relevant information [Ref 2]. Incoming electronic documents must be retained in their original format [Ref 2].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **Poland:** All companies will be required to issue invoices through the KSeF system from January 2024 [Ref 1].
- **Germany:** Mandatory B2B e-invoicing starts January 1, 2025 [Ref 2]. The invoice retention period is reduced from 10 years to 8 years [Ref 2]. New credit note rules are effective from December 6, 2024 [Ref 2].
- **South Africa:** New compliance obligations for electronic document exchange and reporting are anticipated [Ref 3].
- **China:** New compliance requirements for allocating input VAT and anti-avoidance rules are being introduced [Ref 4].
- **Botswana:** Reverse charge VAT will be applied to certain supplies [Ref 6].

## Submission & Interfaces
- **South Africa:** Implementation timelines and requirements to connect to approved service providers may be announced [Ref 3].
- **Germany:** The tax authority can request machine evaluations of the retained data, which must be provided in a machine-readable format [Ref 2].

## Changes vs. Previous
- **Germany:** The second amendment to the GoBD allows businesses to retain only structured e-invoice data (like XML files) if the original data remains unchanged [Ref 2]. Previous court rulings on credit notes are overridden by new rules effective December 6, 2024 [Ref 2].

## Implementation Impact
- **Poland:** Businesses must ensure their systems are compatible with KSeF requirements [Ref 1].
- **Germany:** Businesses must comply with new e-invoicing regulations starting January 1, 2025, including maintaining incoming electronic documents in their original formats [Ref 2].
- **Vanuatu:** The rollout of VSMS is planned between August 2025 and January 2026 [Ref 5].

## Gaps/Unknowns
- South Africa's specific e-invoicing frameworks and technical requirements are not detailed in the source [Ref 3].
- It is unclear if China's requirement for foreign taxpayers leasing property to appoint a domestic agent extends to other taxable services or intangible assets [Ref 4].
- Uncertainty remains about the application of China's anti-avoidance rules to gratuitous services [Ref 4].
