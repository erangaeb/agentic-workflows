## Requirements / Specifications
- A vehicle is considered new if it has travelled less than 6,000 km or has been in use for less than six months [Ref 1].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- The Danish Customs and Tax Administration imposed acquisition VAT on an electric vehicle imported from Germany because it was considered a new means of transport under the VAT Act [Ref 1].
- The right of disposal was transferred to the buyer upon importation, which supported the VAT assessment [Ref 1].
- The court upheld the tax authority’s interpretation, stating that the conditions for exemption were not met and the VAT charge was valid [Ref 1].
- Buyers of vehicles within the EU should take note of the distance driven and duration of use before import to avoid unexpected VAT liabilities [Ref 1].
