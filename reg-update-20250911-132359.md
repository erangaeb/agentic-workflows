# Poland's e-Invoice Regulatory Updates: Preparing for the KSeF Mandate

Poland is progressing with its mandatory B2B e-invoicing system, the National e-Invoice System (KSeF), with the full mandate now set for February 1, 2026 [^1][^2]. Businesses are urged to begin preparations immediately to ensure compliance and avoid potential risks and costs associated with delayed readiness [^1].

## Key Dates and Mandate Overview

The mandatory B2B e-invoicing through KSeF will commence on **February 1, 2026** for Polish VAT taxpayers [^2][^3]. This date follows a postponement from earlier proposed timelines.

Key dates for the transition to KSeF 2.0 include:
*   **September 30, 2025:** KSeF 2.0 test environment becomes available [^3].
*   **November 1, 2025:** New permissions and certificate downloads for KSeF 2.0 will be available via the Central Register and Information on Economic Activity (MCU) [^3].
*   **January 26-31, 2026:** KSeF 1.0 will be available until a technical break during this period [^3].
*   **February 1, 2026:** KSeF 2.0 becomes mandatory [^3].

## KSeF System Evolution: From 1.0 to 2.0

The Ministry of Finance is transitioning from KSeF 1.0 to KSeF 2.0, which introduces significant changes:
*   **API Incompatibility:** KSeF 1.0 and KSeF 2.0 APIs are not compatible, requiring businesses to adapt their systems [^3].
*   **Data Migration:** Only invoices and ownership rights will migrate from KSeF 1.0 to KSeF 2.0 [^3].
*   **Development Focus:** The KSeF 1.0 production version will not be further developed, with all future enhancements focused on KSeF 2.0 [^3].

## Key Features and Changes in KSeF 2.0

KSeF 2.0 aims to provide a more robust and integrated e-invoicing experience:
*   **New Invoice Structure:** It will feature a new invoice structure, FA3, and an improved API designed for better integration with business systems [^3].
*   **Testing Environment:** The test environment, available from September 30, 2025, will allow companies to check authentication, encryption, and access management, ensuring system compliance and optimizing invoicing processes [^3].
*   **Support Resources:** The Ministry will provide comprehensive documentation, code examples, and training to assist businesses with the transition [^3].

## Invoice Correction Procedures in KSeF

The Ministry of Finance has clarified the procedures for correcting errors in KSeF invoices, emphasizing that **there will be no corrective notes** within the system [^4].
*   **Incorrect NIP (Tax Identification Number):** If an invoice contains an incorrect NIP, it should be corrected to zero and then reissued with the correct NIP [^4].
*   **Other Errors:** For other types of errors on an invoice, corrections must be made through a formal invoice correction process [^4].

## Urgent Steps for Businesses to Prepare

Businesses should not view 2026 as a distant deadline, as preparation is a complex, cross-functional effort involving accounting, IT, and logistics [^1]. Key actions include:
*   **Capacity Building:** Develop internal capacity for e-invoice generation and transmission [^1].
*   **Offline Scenarios:** Plan for scenarios where the KSeF system might be temporarily unavailable, ensuring business continuity [^1].
*   **System Adaptation:** Adapt existing IT systems to capture new mandatory data fields required by KSeF [^1].
*   **Compliance Controls:** Strengthen internal controls and validation processes to ensure data accuracy and compliance [^1].
*   **Invoice Verification:** Implement robust invoice checks and identifier systems [^1].
*   **Expense Validation:** Ensure proper validation of employee expenses and alignment of data with KSeF requirements [^1].

Delaying preparation can lead to significant compliance risks, including discrepancies in VAT reporting and issues with VAT deduction [^1].

## Legal Framework

The implementation of mandatory KSeF is based on a key legal act: a regulation by the Minister of Finance and Economy on using KSeF [^2]. The latest draft of this regulation is currently undergoing public consultations and inter-ministerial agreements. This regulation aims to implement the authorization provided in the VAT Act, which has been amended to introduce mandatory KSeF, covering permissions, authentication methods, and invoice marking within the system [^2].
