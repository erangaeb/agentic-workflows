## Topic
- poland e-invoice updates

## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction/scope: Poland [Ref 1].
- Effective/compliance dates: Mandatory e-invoicing in Poland will be introduced from July 1, 2024 [Ref 1]. This date is subject to change [Ref 1].

## Requirements / Specifications
- KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur) is the National e-Invoicing System in Poland [Ref 1].
- Structured e-invoices will be mandatory via KSeF [Ref 1].
- KSeF will use the Peppol ID to identify entities [Ref 2].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- KSeF is intended to tighten VAT collection [Ref 1].

## Submission & Interfaces
- KSeF Platform is used for e-invoicing in Poland [Ref 1].

## Changes vs. Previous
- The mandatory e-invoicing start date was initially planned for January 1, 2024, but has been postponed to July 1, 2024 [Ref 1]. This date is subject to change [Ref 1].
