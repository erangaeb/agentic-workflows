# E-Invoice Regulatory Update – June 2024

This update provides a detailed overview of recent and upcoming regulatory changes in e-invoicing across key European jurisdictions, with references to official sources and industry events.

---

## 1. Poland: National e-Invoice System (KSeF) Mandate in 2026

Poland is set to implement mandatory e-invoicing for B2B transactions via the National e-Invoice System (KSeF) starting February 1, 2026. This marks a significant shift from paper/PDF invoices to structured XML files, aiming to streamline VAT compliance and reporting.

**Key Regulatory Features:**
- **Uniform Data Structure:** All invoices must follow the Ministry of Finance’s XML schema, supporting over 400 fields for customization and automation [ksiegowosc.infor.pl](https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/).
- **Verification Codes:** Invoices issued outside KSeF require a verification code, typically a QR code, for authenticity.
- **Issuance & Delivery:** Submission to KSeF generates a unique ID and timestamp, establishing the official issuance and delivery date.
- **Visualization:** While XML is the official format, accounting systems can generate readable previews (PDF/table) for informational purposes.
- **Transition to KSeF 2.0:** New API, permissions, and test environment available from September 30, 2025. KSeF 1.0 will be phased out by January 31, 2026 [gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/ksef).

**Correction of Errors:**
- Incorrect NIP (Tax ID) must be set to zero and the invoice reissued.
- Other errors require a correction invoice; no corrective notes are permitted in KSeF [podatki.gazetaprawna.pl](https://podatki.gazetaprawna.pl/).

**Business Readiness:**
- Companies must adapt IT and accounting systems, validate new data fields, and prepare for offline scenarios.
- Early preparation is advised to avoid compliance risks and VAT deduction issues [fintua.com](https://fintua.com/).

## 2. Luxembourg: E-Invoicing in Public Procurement

Luxembourg has mandated e-invoicing for public procurement since March 18, 2023, in line with EU Directive 2014/55/EU.

**Key Regulatory Features:**
- **Scope:** All suppliers to public entities must issue e-invoices compliant with EN 16931.
- **Technical Standards:** Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 and UBL 2.1 formats are required.
- **Network:** Peppol is the backbone for B2G e-invoicing; monthly reporting by service providers is required since January 2024.
- **No B2B/B2C Mandate:** E-invoicing is not yet mandatory for private sector transactions.
- **Future Alignment:** Legislation is planned to align with the EU’s VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) directive by July 2030 [fiscal-requirements.com](https://fiscal-requirements.com/).

## 3. Germany: E-Invoicing Guideline and EN 16931 Alignment

Germany has published a new guideline mapping VAT law requirements to the EU e-invoice standard EN 16931.

**Key Regulatory Features:**
- **Guideline Developed By:** XStandards Einkauf, FeRD, and AWV.
- **Purpose:** Ensures interoperability and compliance for businesses and software providers.
- **Technical Mapping:** Provides detailed mapping between German VAT Act and EN 16931 fields.
- **Updates:** Regular updates are planned to reflect regulatory changes [fiscal-requirements.com](https://fiscal-requirements.com/).

## 4. Italy: E-Invoicing Compliance and SDI Platform Evolution

Italy remains the EU’s pioneer in e-invoicing, with mandatory use of the SDI platform for B2B, B2C, and B2G invoices since 2019.

**Key Regulatory Features:**
- **Structured XML Format:** All invoices must be submitted in FatturaPA XML via SDI.
- **Coverage:** All resident VAT taxpayers; non-resident VAT-only registrants report cross-border flows but are exempt from SDI for domestic invoices.
- **Technical Updates:** FatturaPA v1.9 (2025) introduces new codes (TD29, RF20) and technical specifications.
- **Deadlines:** Invoices must be transmitted within 12 days of the transaction; deferred invoices by the 15th of the following month.
- **Archiving:** Electronic storage for 10 years is mandatory (“conservazione sostitutiva”).
- **EU Alignment:** Italy’s system serves as a blueprint for the EU’s ViDA initiative, with the current derogation expiring in December 2027 unless harmonized EU-wide rules are adopted [Global VAT Compliance](https://www.globalvatcompliance.com/globalvatnews/italy-e-invoicing-compliance-2025/).

## 5. EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) Initiative

The EU’s ViDA proposal aims to harmonize e-invoicing and digital VAT reporting across member states by 2030.

**Key Regulatory Features:**
- **Structured Invoice Formats:** EN 16931 compliance will be required EU-wide.
- **Centralized Clearance:** Real-time or near real-time reporting is expected to become the norm.
- **Progressive Removal of Exemptions:** Micro-entities and special regimes will be brought into scope.
- **Cross-Border Interoperability:** Enhanced support for cross-border transactions and reporting.

**Industry Events:**
