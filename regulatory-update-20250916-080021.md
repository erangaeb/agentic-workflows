## Topic
- e-invoice regulatory updates poland.

## Document Metadata
- **Jurisdiction/scope:** Poland [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4]. The updates concern the National e-Invoicing System (KSeF) [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
- **Publishing authority:** Polish Ministry of Finance [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
- **Version/document date:** The announcement regarding the postponement was made on 26 April 2024 [Ref 1]. An audit of the KSeF system concluded in April 2024 [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
- **Effective/compliance dates (incl. grace periods):**
    - The new mandatory effective date for most taxpayers is 1 February 2026 [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
    - For small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) exempt from VAT, the mandatory date is 1 April 2026 [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
    - Voluntary use of KSeF remains possible [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].

## Requirements / Specifications
- The sources indicate that the KSeF system's architecture and code require a thorough re-design and re-implementation [Ref 1].
- Public consultations on the new KSeF system architecture and proposed solutions are planned for the second half of 2024 [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
- A new draft law is expected to be published in the coming months, which may introduce changes to the system's functionalities [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].

## Validation / Business Rules
- An audit revealed critical errors and significant deficiencies in the KSeF system's architecture and code [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4]. These deficiencies could hinder the system's stability and efficiency [Ref 1]. The audit concluded that the system was not ready for mandatory implementation [Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- The National e-Invoicing System (KSeF) is Poland's e-invoicing platform [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
- The mandatory scope applies to most taxpayers, with a specific later date for VAT-exempt SMEs [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].

## Submission & Interfaces
- No specific details on API endpoints, authentication, or submission channels are provided in the context of the *new* system architecture, as it is still under re-design [Ref 1].

## Changes vs. Previous
- The mandatory implementation of KSeF has been postponed from its previously planned date [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
- The postponement is a direct result of an audit that identified critical errors in the KSeF system's architecture and code [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
- The Ministry of Finance plans to conduct public consultations on the new KSeF system architecture and proposed solutions in the second half of 2024 [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].
- A new legislative proposal is anticipated to be published in the coming months to formalize the revised implementation dates and potential system modifications [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].

## Implementation Impact
- The identified critical errors necessitate a thorough re-design and re-implementation of the KSeF system [Ref 1].
- Taxpayers should anticipate potential changes to the system's functionalities once the new draft law and architecture are finalized [Ref 1].
- Businesses currently using KSeF voluntarily can continue to do so [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4].

## Gaps/Unknowns
- Specific technical requirements (e.g., XML schema, UBL version, data fields, API specifications) for the re-designed KSeF system are not yet available [Ref 1].

- The exact timeline for the publication of the new draft law and the commencement of public consultations beyond "coming months" and "second half of 2024" is not provided [Ref 1].
- Any specific penalties or enforcement mechanisms for non-compliance under the new timeline are not mentioned [Ref 1].
