## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **TOGC Exemption:** The Swedish VAT Act requires the transferee to be entitled to deduct input VAT for the transfer of a going concern (TOGC) exemption to apply [Ref 1]. The exemption does not apply if the transferee cannot deduct input VAT [Ref 1]. This may impact the transfer of VAT-exempt and mixed businesses, potentially causing VAT leakage [Ref 1].
- **Food VAT Reduction:** The Swedish government plans to reduce the VAT rate on food from 12% to 6% [Ref 2]. This change is proposed to be effective from April 1, 2026, until January 1, 2028 [Ref 2].
- **VAT on EV Charging:** The place of supply for electricity used in EV charging within Sweden is Sweden, as the consumption occurs domestically [Ref 3]. A fixed fee for electronic access to charging station networks is also subject to Swedish VAT if the customers are non-taxable persons resident or habitually residing in Sweden [Ref 3].
