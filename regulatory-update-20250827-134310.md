## Format Requirements
- Italy: FatturaPA XML format aligned with European Standard EN 16931 [Ref 7].
- Germany: Structured XML formats are mandated for B2B e-invoicing [Ref 5].

## Validation Rules
- Italy: The SDI system performs format checks and supports national CIUS specifications [Ref 7].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- Romania: Aims to enhance transparency and reduce tax evasion [Ref 14].
- Pakistan: Aims to enhance compliance, enable real-time sales tax reporting, and reduce VAT leakage [Ref 3].
- Italy: Enhances VAT compliance and fraud prevention through near real-time transmission of invoice data to the Revenue Agency [Ref 7].
- ViDA: To simplify VAT compliance across EU Member States and to reduce VAT fraud through real-time digital reporting [Ref 9].

## Submission & Reporting
- Italy: All invoices must be submitted via the central Sistema di Interscambio (SDI) platform [Ref 7]. Real-time VAT reporting via SDI [Ref 7].
- Pakistan: Businesses must complete integration with FBR systems via licensed integrators [Ref 3].
- ViDA: Real-time digital reporting of invoice data to national tax authorities [Ref 9]. Tax authorities will share data via a centralized EU system [Ref 9].

## Regulatory Changes (Diff vs. previous)
- Germany: Reduced the statutory invoice retention period from 10 years to 8 years [Ref 5].
- Germany: New credit note rules effective December 6, 2024, regarding unauthorized tax liability [Ref 5].

## Implementation Impact
- Romania: Businesses will need to ensure their invoicing systems are compliant with the new regulations, which may involve updating software and training staff [Ref 14].
- Pakistan: Companies are advised to assess their categories based on turnover, upgrade their systems for FBR integration, utilize the testing phase to resolve issues, and train staff on new processes [Ref 3].
- Côte d’Ivoire: Companies must choose their issuance channels (API, web portal, mobile app, or electronic payment terminals) and ensure compliance through proper data mapping, testing, and stakeholder training [Ref 4].
