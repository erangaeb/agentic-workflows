## Topic
- regulatory updates

## Document Metadata
- Jurisdictions covered: Poland, Germany, South Africa, China, Vanuatu, Botswana. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6]

## Requirements / Specifications
- **Germany:** Structured XML formats are mandated for B2B e-invoicing. [Ref 2]
- **South Africa:** E-invoice is defined as a tax invoice in a structured electronic format for automatic processing. [Ref 3]

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **Poland:** Mandatory e-invoicing through the KSeF system starts in January 2024 for all companies. [Ref 1]
- **Germany:** Mandatory B2B e-invoicing starts January 1, 2025 [Ref 2]. The invoice retention period is reduced from 10 years to 8 years [Ref 2]. New credit note rules are effective from December 6, 2024 [Ref 2].
- **South Africa:** The draft bill proposes amendments to the Value-Added Tax Act [Ref 3]. E-reporting involves electronically submitting tax data from e-invoices, e-debit notes, and e-credit notes [Ref 3].
- **China:** Draft VAT implementation regulations were released on August 11, 2025, with public consultation open until September 10, 2025 [Ref 4]. New compliance requirements for allocating input VAT and anti-avoidance rules are introduced [Ref 4].
- **Vanuatu:** Implementing e-invoicing to improve tax compliance and reduce the tax gap [Ref 5]. The rollout of VSMS is planned between August 2025 and January 2026 [Ref 5].
- **Botswana:** Proposes VAT amendment to tax digital services from non-residents [Ref 6]. Reverse charge VAT to be applied to certain supplies [Ref 6].

## Validation / Business Rules
- **Germany:** If a non-entrepreneurial person issues a credit note with VAT and does not promptly object, it may trigger unauthorized tax liability, overriding previous court rulings on credit notes [Ref 2].

## Submission & Interfaces
- **South Africa:** Interoperability Framework involves a network for decentralized exchange of electronic documents [Ref 3].

## Changes vs. Previous
- **Germany:** The second amendment to the GoBD allows businesses to retain only structured e-invoice data (like XML files) if the original data remains unchanged, while human-readable formats (e.g., PDFs) are only required if they contain additional tax-relevant information [Ref 2].

## Implementation Impact
- **Poland:** Businesses must ensure their systems are compatible with KSeF requirements [Ref 1].
- **Germany:** Businesses must comply with new e-invoicing regulations starting January 1, 2025, including maintaining incoming electronic documents in their original formats and ensuring that any converted formats preserve the original structured data [Ref 2].
- **South Africa:** New compliance obligations for electronic document exchange and reporting are anticipated [Ref 3].

## Gaps/Unknowns
- South Africa: Implementation timelines and requirements to connect to approved service providers may be announced [Ref 3].
- China: It is unclear if the requirement for foreign taxpayers leasing property in China to appoint a domestic agent for VAT obligations extends to other taxable services or intangible assets [Ref 4]. Uncertainty remains about the application of anti-avoidance rules to gratuitous services [Ref 4].
