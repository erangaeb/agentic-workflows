# Poland's eInvoicing Mandate: Key Updates and Preparations for 2026

Poland's journey towards mandatory B2B eInvoicing through the National e-Invoice System (KSeF) continues, with significant developments regarding its implementation, system updates, and error correction procedures. Businesses must take urgent steps to prepare for the revised mandatory start date in February 2026.

---

## Mandatory KSeF Implementation Postponed to February 2026

The mandatory implementation of the National e-Invoice System (KSeF) for Polish VAT taxpayers has been postponed. The new effective date for mandatory e-invoicing through KSeF is **February 2026** [Source: ksiegowosc.infor.pl]. This delay provides businesses with additional time to adapt their systems and processes, but delaying preparation can lead to significant compliance risks and costs [Source: fintua.com].

---

## Transition to KSeF 2.0: New Features and Testing Environment

The Ministry of Finance is preparing for the transition to KSeF 2.0, which will introduce new functionalities and an improved technical architecture.

*   **Incompatibility:** KSeF 1.0 and KSeF 2.0 APIs are **not compatible** [Source: gov.pl].
*   **Data Migration:** Only invoices and ownership rights will migrate from KSeF 1.0 to KSeF 2.0 [Source: gov.pl].
*   **New Invoice Structure:** KSeF 2.0 will feature a new invoice structure, **FA3**, and an improved API designed for better integration [Source: gov.pl].
*   **Test Environment:** An efficient test environment for KSeF 2.0 is being prepared by the Ministry of Finance and will be available from **September 30, 2025** [Source: gov.pl]. This environment will allow businesses to check authentication, encryption, and access management, verify system compliance, and optimize invoicing processes [Source: gov.pl].
*   **KSeF 1.0 Sunset:** The KSeF 1.0 production version will not be further developed and will remain available until a technical break scheduled from January 26-31, 2026 [Source: gov.pl].
*   **Permissions and Certificates:** New permissions and certificate downloads for KSeF 2.0 will be available via the Central Register of Authorizations (MCU) from **November 1, 2025** [Source: gov.pl]. The Ministry will provide documentation, code examples, and training to support the transition [Source: gov.pl].

## Correcting Errors in KSeF Invoices

The Ministry of Finance has clarified procedures for correcting errors within the KSeF system, addressing concerns about invoice amendments.

*   **Incorrect NIP (Tax Identification Number):** If an invoice contains an incorrect NIP, it should be **corrected to zero**, and the invoice must be **reissued with the correct NIP** [Source: podatki.gazetaprawna.pl].
*   **Other Errors:** For other types of errors on an invoice, corrections should be made through an **invoice correction** [Source: podatki.gazetaprawna.pl].
*   **No Corrective Notes:** It is crucial to note that **corrective notes will not be available** in the KSeF system; errors must be corrected as per the stated procedures [Source: podatki.gazetaprawna.pl].

## Urgent Steps for Businesses: Preparing for the 2026 Mandate

Businesses should not treat 2026 as a distant deadline. Proactive preparation is essential to mitigate compliance risks and avoid increased costs.

*   **Cross-functional Readiness:** E-invoicing readiness involves collaboration across accounting, IT, and logistics departments [Source: fintua.com].
*   **Capacity Building:** Plan for building capacity for invoice generation and transmission through KSeF [Source: fintua.com].
*   **Offline Scenarios:** Develop plans for offline scenarios in case of system downtime to ensure business continuity [Source: fintua.com].
*   **System Adaptation:** Adapt existing systems to capture new mandatory data fields required by KSeF [Source: fintua.com].
*   **Compliance Controls:** Strengthen controls and validation processes for compliance, including implementing invoice checks and identifier systems [Source: fintua.com].
*   **Data Alignment:** Validate employee expenses and ensure data alignment across all financial processes [Source: fintua.com].
