## Tax & VAT Compliance
- Virginia's sales tax rate is 5.3% [Ref 1].
- An additional 1% regional tax applies in Northern Virginia and Hampton Roads [Ref 1].
- This results in a total sales tax of 6.3% in those regions [Ref 1].
- Certain localities may also impose an additional tax of up to 1%, bringing the maximum possible sales tax to 7.3% [Ref 1].
- Virginia has several sales tax holidays [Ref 2]:
    - A three-day sales tax holiday for hurricane preparedness equipment, typically held in late May [Ref 2].
    - A three-day sales tax holiday for school supplies, clothing, and footwear, typically held in early August [Ref 2].
    - A permanent sales tax exemption for eligible energy-efficient appliances bearing the Energy Star label [Ref 2].
