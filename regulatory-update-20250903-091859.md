## Document Metadata

## Requirements / Specifications
- All companies in Denmark are required to send e-invoices to public authorities [Ref 1].
- E-invoices must be sent according to the OIOUBL standard [Ref 1].
- The OIOUBL standard is based on the international standard, Universal Business Language (UBL) [Ref 1].
- E-invoices must be sent via the NemHandel infrastructure [Ref 1].
- NemHandel is the Danish e-invoicing infrastructure [Ref 1].
