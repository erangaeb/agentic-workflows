## Topic
- e-invoice updates hungary.

## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction/scope: Hungary. [Ref 1]
- Publishing authority: NAV (Hungary’s Tax Authority). [Ref 1]
- Effective/compliance dates: September 15, 2025. [Ref 1]

## Requirements / Specifications
- NAV will reclassify 15 warning messages as error messages in the online invoice reporting system [Ref 1].
- Submissions that trigger these errors will be automatically rejected [Ref 1].
- Companies must review their XML data submissions [Ref 1].

## Validation / Business Rules
- Previous warnings will now prevent invoice acceptance [Ref 1].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- The changes aim to enhance data quality and compliance, supporting the effectiveness of the eVAT system [Ref 1].

## Implementation Impact
- Companies are encouraged to conduct regular internal audits and proactively address warning messages to mitigate risks and ensure accurate reporting [Ref 1].

## Compliance and Penalties
- Businesses face penalties of up to HUF 1 million per invoice for incorrect or incomplete submissions [Ref 1].

## Gaps/Unknowns
- The specific 15 warning messages being reclassified as errors are not listed [Ref 1].
