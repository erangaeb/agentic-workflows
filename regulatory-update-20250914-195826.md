## Tax & VAT Compliance
- As of January 1, 2024, Hungary's VAT rate for catering services has increased from 5% to 18% [Ref 1]. This change impacts restaurants and other establishments providing catering services [Ref 1].
- The reduced VAT rate of 5% still applies to specific services, including:
  - Books, newspapers, and periodicals [Ref 1]
  - Medicines and medical equipment [Ref 1]
  - Accommodation services [Ref 1]
  - Entrance fees to cultural events [Ref 1]
