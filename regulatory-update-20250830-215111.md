## Topic
- spain e-invoice regulatory updates

## Document Metadata (if applicable to the topic)
- Jurisdiction/scope: EU, Poland, Slovakia, Belgium, Bulgaria, Pakistan, Egypt, Oman [Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 10][Ref 11][Ref 12].

## Requirements / Specifications (topic-specific)
- Slovakia's e-invoicing system will be based on the EU’s ViDA Directive and Peppol model [Ref 3].
- Slovakia's draft law defines electronic invoices as structured documents compliant with the European EN 16931 standard [Ref 3].
- Bulgaria's SAF-T reporting requires specific XML formats [Ref 5].
- Poland's KSeF system mandates structured e-invoices [Ref 7].
- Oman is likely to adopt the PEPPOL 5-corner model [Ref 12].

## Validation / Business Rules (if relevant)
- Poland's KSeF involves submission, validation, and storage of e-invoices [Ref 7].

## Tax & VAT Compliance (if relevant)
- New EU VAT rules for e-commerce and marketplaces will be effective from July 1, 2028 [Ref 2].
- Online sellers and marketplaces will be responsible for collecting VAT on imported goods [Ref 2].
- Sellers and marketplaces will pay VAT in the final destination state instead of the consumer [Ref 2].
- Foreign suppliers not registered with IOSS must pay import VAT and register in each destination state [Ref 2].
- Pakistan's electronic invoicing aims to ensure real-time sales tax reporting and reduce VAT leakage [Ref 10].

## Submission & Interfaces (if relevant)
- Poland's KSeF is a teleinformatics system for issuing, receiving, and archiving structured invoices [Ref 9].
- Pakistan requires businesses to issue e-invoices via digitally linked software for direct reporting to FBR [Ref 10].
- Egypt emphasizes compliance with technical requirements and use of online resources for e-receipts [Ref 11].

## Changes vs. Previous (if the topic is an update)
- Poland postponed the initial KSeF implementation planned for July 2024 to 2026 [Ref 7].
- Belgium postponed the VAT chain rollout and extended the time for e-invoicing implementation [Ref 4].

## Implementation Impact
- Slovakia: Businesses should prepare for system integration and compliance simplification [Ref 3].
- Poland: Implementation of KSeF requires adjustments in accounting systems and inter-departmental collaboration [Ref 9].
- Pakistan: Businesses must integrate and register with FBR systems before their go-live date [Ref 10]. Businesses should assess their category, upgrade systems, test early, and train staff [Ref 10].
