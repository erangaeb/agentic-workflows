## Topic
- Denmark e‑invoice compliance updates.

## Requirements / Specifications
- EN 16931 is the European standard for e-invoicing, designed to ensure interoperability across EU member states by standardizing the data model for core e-invoice elements [Ref 1].
- An EN 16931-compliant invoice includes header information, seller details, buyer details, invoice line items, tax details, totals and summaries, and payment terms and information [Ref 1].
- Approved technical formats for EN 16931 implementation are UBL (Universal Business Language) and UN/CEFACT’s CII (Cross Industry Invoice), both XML-based [Ref 1].

## Submission & Interfaces
- The Peppol network is a secure network for exchanging business documents, including EN 16931-compliant e-invoices, and uses EN 16931 as the content standard for its invoices [Ref 1].

## Validation / Business Rules
- A CIUS (Core Invoice Usage Specification) is a country- or sector-specific adaptation of EN 16931 that refines the standard for local requirements, specifying which data elements are mandatory or optional [Ref 1].

## Implementation Impact
- Complexities remain due to country-specific CIUS and Extensions, requiring businesses to adapt to varying national requirements [Ref 1].
