## Topic
- e-invoice updates

## Document Metadata
- Scope: Global updates on e-invoicing and e-reporting mandates and implementations. [Ref 1]

## Requirements / Specifications
- **Poland KSeF**: Structured invoices are issued, received, and archived in a teleinformatics system [Ref 5].

## Validation / Business Rules
- **Hungary**: Starting September 15, 2025, Hungary's Tax Authority (NAV) will reclassify 15 warning messages as error messages in the online invoice reporting system, leading to automatic rejections of submissions that trigger these errors [Ref 16]. Businesses face penalties of up to HUF 1 million per invoice for incorrect or incomplete submissions [Ref 16].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **Colombia**: E-invoices must include a QR code and a Unique Code of Electronic Invoice (CUFE) for verification purposes [Ref 16]. All invoices must be archived for five years [Ref 16].
- **Philippines**: Non-resident digital service providers must register for VAT, file quarterly returns, and may be audited using payment data [Ref 15]. Penalties for non-compliance include fines, domain blocking, and business suspension [Ref 15]. Quarterly VAT returns are due by the 25th day of the month following each quarter [Ref 15].
- **Romania**: Fines for late or missing transmission of e-invoices range from RON 5,000–10,000 for large taxpayers, RON 2,500–5,000 for medium taxpayers, and RON 1,000–2,500 for small taxpayers [Ref 24]. B2B fines are in force since April 2025, and B2C fines are in force since July 2025 [Ref 24].

## Submission & Interfaces
- **Romania**: The RO e-Factura system operates through the SPV platform [Ref 24]. Invoices must be submitted within 5 calendar days from issuance [Ref 24]. Buyers can retrieve invoices for 60 days [Ref 24].

## Changes vs. Previous
- **Panama**: New rules apply from January 1, 2026, where only taxpayers with annual gross revenue up to USD 36,000 and a monthly limit of 100 issued documents are eligible for the free e-invoicing platform [Ref 8]. Previously, the service was available to those with annual revenue up to USD 1,000,000 and unlimited document issuance [Ref 8].
- **Hungary**: Starting September 15, 2025, 15 warning messages will be reclassified as error messages in the online invoice reporting system, leading to automatic rejections [Ref 16].

## Implementation Impact
- **Poland**: Implementation of KSeF requires adjustments in accounting systems and inter-departmental collaboration [Ref 5].
- **Croatia**: By December 31, 2025, businesses must implement compliant invoicing software and register their e-Invoice address [Ref 14].

## Gaps/Unknowns
- Specific details regarding the turnover threshold for mandatory e-invoicing in South Africa [Ref 22].
