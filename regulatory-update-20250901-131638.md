## Topic
- e-invoicing updates

## Document Metadata
- **Last update:** August 31, 2025 [Ref 3]

## Requirements / Specifications
- **UAE:** E-invoicing requires uploading 13 aspects of a tax invoice [Ref 2].
- **Slovakia:** Electronic invoices must comply with the European EN 16931 standard [Ref 21].
- **Romania:** E-invoices must be in RO_CIUS (UBL/CII) format, aligned with EN 16931 [Ref 24]. Consumer IDs can be replaced with zeros for privacy [Ref 24].

## Validation / Business Rules
- **UAE:** Service providers will validate invoices and inform the FTA [Ref 2].
- **Romania:** Companies need pre-validation and reject-handling loops [Ref 24]. Invalid VAT IDs or addresses now block invoice validation [Ref 24].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **South Africa:** Plans to amend VAT Law to include e-invoicing and e-reporting [Ref 1].
- **UAE:** All VAT-registered businesses must use e-invoicing for transactions with other registered entities [Ref 2].
- **Romania:** Since January 1, 2024, B2B e-invoicing is mandatory for Romanian-established businesses and non-established VAT-registered taxpayers supplying goods/services with place of supply in Romania [Ref 24]. Since January 1, 2025, the scope expanded to cover B2C transactions [Ref 24]. B2G invoicing has been mandatory since 2022 [Ref 24].
- **Malaysia:** Mandatory e-invoicing applies from July 1, 2025, for taxpayers with annual income between 5 million and 25 million ringgits [Ref 25][Ref 26].
- **Poland:** Invoices for agricultural products from flat-rate farmers are exempt [Ref 16].

## Submission & Interfaces
- **UAE:** Businesses must map their accounting software to licensed service providers [Ref 2].
- **Romania:** The RO e-Factura system operates through the SPV platform or APIs [Ref 24]. The submission deadline is 5 calendar days from issuance [Ref 24]. Buyers can retrieve invoices for 60 days [Ref 24].
- **Croatia:** E-Invoices will be exchanged between businesses and government entities through access points [Ref 22]. Access points must meet technical requirements for secure data transfer and interoperability [Ref 22].

## Changes vs. Previous
- **Poland:** Corrective notes will no longer be used with the mandatory KSeF system [Ref 13]. Article 106k of VAT law will be repealed on February 1, 2026 [Ref 13].

## Implementation Impact
- **South Africa:** Businesses should monitor regulatory changes and assess current systems [Ref 1].
- **UAE:** Businesses must map their accounting software to licensed service providers [Ref 2].
- **Poland:** Implementation requires changes in accounting and IT systems [Ref 10]. New procedures for invoice circulation and employee training are needed [Ref 10]. Contracts with accounting offices and agreements with contractors must be updated [Ref 10].
- **Romania:** Companies need pre-validation, reject-handling loops, and fallback PDFs to avoid cash collection delays [Ref 24]. Companies need to train AR/AP teams on the 5-day deadline and retrieval/delivery requirements [Ref 24].
- **Croatia:** By December 31, 2025, businesses must implement compliant invoicing software and register their e-Invoice address [Ref 22]. Companies must secure compliant software, contract an intermediary, and register their e-Invoice address [Ref 22].

## Gaps/Unknowns
- South Africa: Technical specifications for e-invoicing systems to be determined [Ref 1].
- UAE: Details about volunteering for a phase of e-invoicing implementation are unclear [Ref 2]. Questions remain about data traffic management and cost structures [Ref 2].
- Greece: Mandatory B2B E-Invoicing date is unclear [Ref 3].
