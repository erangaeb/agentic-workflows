## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction/scope: Brazil [Ref 1]

## Requirements / Specifications
- Brazil will implement a national electronic service invoice standard, NFS-e [Ref 1].
- The new standard replaces various municipal formats [Ref 1].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- Municipalities will have improved revenue control and transparency [Ref 1].
- Failure to adopt the standard may result in loss of Union transfers [Ref 1].

## Implementation Impact
- Businesses will experience simplified processes and potentially lower costs [Ref 1].
