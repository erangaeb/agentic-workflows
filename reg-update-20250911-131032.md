# Poland's KSeF e-Invoicing Mandate: Key Updates, Transition to KSeF 2.0, and Business Readiness for February 2026

Poland is moving forward with its mandatory B2B e-invoicing system, KSeF (National e-Invoice System), with the full implementation now set for **February 1, 2026** for all VAT taxpayers [4]. This update outlines the critical steps businesses must take, the technical evolution of the KSeF system, and the latest guidance on error correction.

## Key Dates and System Transition

The transition to mandatory e-invoicing through KSeF involves a significant system upgrade from KSeF 1.0 to KSeF 2.0, with specific timelines for testing and deployment:

*   **KSeF 2.0 Test Environment Availability:** From **September 30, 2025**, businesses can access the KSeF 2.0 test environment. This is crucial for checking authentication, encryption, and access management, allowing companies to verify system compliance and optimize their invoicing processes [3].
*   **New Permissions and Certificate Downloads (MCU):** From **November 1, 2025**, new permissions and certificate downloads for KSeF 2.0 will be available via the Central Register of Authorizations (MCU) [3].
*   **KSeF 1.0 Decommissioning:** The KSeF 1.0 production version will not be further developed and will remain available only until a technical break scheduled from **January 26-31, 2026** [3].
*   **Mandatory KSeF 2.0 Implementation:** KSeF 2.0 becomes mandatory for all Polish VAT taxpayers from **February 1, 2026** [3, 4].

**Important Note on System Compatibility:** KSeF 1.0 and KSeF 2.0 APIs are **not compatible**. Only invoices and ownership rights will migrate from KSeF 1.0 to KSeF 2.0 [3]. KSeF 2.0 introduces a new invoice structure (FA3) and an improved API for better integration [3].

## Operational Readiness and Business Impact

Businesses are strongly advised against delaying preparation, as readiness involves significant changes across accounting, IT, and logistics departments [1].

Key actions for businesses include:

*   **Capacity Building:** Develop robust capacity for invoice generation and transmission through KSeF [1].
*   **Offline Scenarios:** Plan for offline scenarios to ensure business continuity in case of system downtime [1].
*   **Data Field Adaptation:** Adapt existing systems to capture new mandatory data fields required by KSeF [1].
*   **Strengthen Controls:** Implement and strengthen controls and validation processes for compliance, including invoice checks and identifier systems [1].
*   **Data Alignment:** Validate employee expenses and ensure data alignment across all relevant systems [1].

Compliance risks include discrepancies in data and potential issues with VAT deduction if not properly managed [1].

## Error Correction Procedures in KSeF

The Ministry of Finance has clarified the procedures for correcting errors in KSeF invoices, emphasizing that **corrective notes will not be available** in the system [2].

*   **Incorrect NIP (Tax Identification Number):** If an invoice contains an incorrect NIP, it should be corrected to zero and the invoice reissued with the correct NIP [2].
*   **Other Errors:** For other types of errors on an invoice, corrections should be made through an invoice correction [2].

## Technical Requirements and Integration Guidelines

The Ministry of Finance is actively preparing an efficient test environment for KSeF 2.0 and providing comprehensive support for integration:

*   **Integration with Test Environment:** Companies are required to integrate with the KSeF 2.0 test environment, available from September 30, 2025 [3].
*   **Documentation and Support:** The Ministry provides documentation, code examples, and training to assist businesses with the integration process [3].
*   **Authentication and Permissions:** The new regulation will cover permissions, authentication methods, and invoice marking within KSeF [4].

Businesses can report issues with KSeF 1.0 to [email protected] [3].

## Conclusion

The mandatory implementation of KSeF 2.0 in February 2026 represents a significant shift in Poland's B2B invoicing landscape. Proactive preparation, including system adaptation, process re-engineering, and thorough testing in the KSeF 2.0 environment, is essential to ensure compliance and avoid operational disruptions. Businesses should treat the 2026 deadline as an immediate priority, not a distant future event.
