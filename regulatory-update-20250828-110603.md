## Topic
- e-invoice regulatory updates.

## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction/scope: Global. [Ref 1]
- Publishing authority: EY, Sovos, RTCsuite, dti.rs, Benelux.int, FBR (Pakistan), FIRS (Nigeria), ANAF (Romania). [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 6][Ref 7][Ref 10][Ref 11][Ref 12][Ref 13]
- Version/document date: August 2025. [Ref 1]

## Requirements / Specifications
- **Germany:** Structured XML formats are mandated for B2B e-invoicing. [Ref 7]
- **Romania:** Uses RO_CIUS (UBL/CII) format aligned with EN 16931. [Ref 13]

## Validation / Business Rules
- **Romania:** Invalid VAT IDs or addresses will block invoice validation. [Ref 13]

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **Finland:** Plans to implement VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) for cross-border transactions by 2030. [Ref 1]
- **Germany:** VAT regulations have been updated to mandate structured XML formats for B2B e-invoicing. [Ref 7]
- **Pakistan:** Aims to enhance compliance, enable real-time sales tax reporting, and reduce VAT leakage by integrating business invoicing systems directly with the FBR. [Ref 6]
- **Romania:** Buyers are increasingly refusing invoices not issued via RO e-Factura, as these may jeopardize VAT deduction. [Ref 13]

## Submission & Interfaces
- **Oman:** Businesses must ensure their systems are integrated with the tax authority’s platform for real-time invoice submission. [Ref 4]
- **Romania:** Operates through the ANAF SPV portal or APIs. [Ref 13]
- **Romania:** Submission deadline is 5 calendar days from issuance. [Ref 13]

## Changes vs. Previous
- **Germany:** Reduced the statutory invoice retention period from 10 years to 8 years. [Ref 7]

## Implementation Impact
- **Croatia:** VAT-registered businesses must implement mandatory e-invoicing for all domestic B2B transactions. [Ref 1]
- **Germany:** Businesses must comply with new e-invoicing regulations, which include maintaining incoming electronic documents in their original formats and ensuring that any converted formats preserve the original structured data. [Ref 7]
- **North Macedonia:** Businesses are required to adapt their accounting systems to comply with the new e-invoicing regulations, ensuring that invoices are issued electronically and transmitted to the tax authority in real-time. [Ref 3]
- **Pakistan:** Companies are advised to assess their categories based on turnover, upgrade their systems for FBR integration, utilize the testing phase to resolve issues, and train staff on new processes to ensure a smooth transition before the deadlines. [Ref 6]
- **Poland:** Businesses must act swiftly to ensure their systems are compatible with KSeF requirements. [Ref 5]
- **Romania:** Companies need pre-validation, reject-handling loops, and fallback PDFs to avoid cash collection delays. [Ref 13]
- **Romania:** Contracts should specify e-Factura responsibilities, particularly around buyer retrieval and invoice delivery. [Ref 13]

## Gaps/Unknowns
- Cambodia, Malaysia, and Romania were added in the June 11 update, but no specific details were provided regarding their e-invoicing developments [Ref 1].
- Specific technical requirements for connecting to approved service providers in South Africa are not detailed [Ref 2].
- Deadlines for the selection process or implementation of Oman's e-invoicing initiative are not specified [Ref 4].
