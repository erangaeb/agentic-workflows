## Topic

## Requirements / Specifications
- A vehicle is considered new if it has traveled less than 6,000 km or has been in use for less than six months [Ref 1].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- The Danish National Tax Court clarified the VAT treatment for imported vehicles under the Danish VAT Act [Ref 1].
- The Danish Customs and Tax Administration imposed acquisition VAT on an electric vehicle imported from Germany because it met the criteria of a new means of transport under the applicable VAT legislation [Ref 1].
- The right of disposal was transferred to the buyer upon importation, which supported the assessment of VAT [Ref 1].
- The court upheld the tax authority’s interpretation that the conditions for exemption were not met and the VAT charge was valid [Ref 1].
- Buyers of vehicles within the EU should take note of the distance driven and duration of use before import to avoid unexpected VAT liabilities [Ref 1].

## Gaps/Unknowns
- The specific VAT rate applied is not mentioned [Ref 1].
- The exact criteria in the Danish VAT Act that define a "new means of transport" are not quoted [Ref 1].
