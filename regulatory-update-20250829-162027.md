## Topic
- e-invoice updates spain

## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction/scope: Spain. [Ref 1]

## Requirements / Specifications
- Spain is developing regulations for mandatory B2B e-invoicing [Ref 1].
- The regulation will require that all companies and the self-employed issue, send, and receive electronic invoices in their business relations [Ref 1].
- The e-invoices must guarantee integrity, authenticity, and traceability [Ref 1].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- The Spanish State Tax Administration Agency (AEAT) will have access to the invoices [Ref 1].
- The e-invoicing system aims to improve VAT control and reduce fraud [Ref 1].

## Implementation Impact
- Companies will need to adapt their systems to issue and receive electronic invoices [Ref 1].
