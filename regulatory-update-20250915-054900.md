## Topic
- Poland e-invoice updates.

## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction: Poland. [Ref 1]

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- As of February 2024, Poland has postponed the mandatory e-invoicing mandate until further notice [Ref 1]. A new date will be determined after further analysis and consultation with businesses [Ref 1].
- The original effective date for mandatory e-invoicing in Poland was July 1, 2024 [Ref 2]. This date has been postponed [Ref 2].
- The KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur) is the national e-invoicing system in Poland [Ref 2].
- The Polish Ministry of Finance is responsible for the KSeF e-invoicing system [Ref 2].

## Gaps/Unknowns
- The new effective date for mandatory e-invoicing in Poland is not specified in the provided sources [Ref 1].
