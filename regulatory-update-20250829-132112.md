## Tax & VAT Compliance
- The Danish National Tax Court clarified the VAT treatment for imported vehicles under the Danish VAT Act [Ref 1].
- Acquisition VAT is imposed on new means of transport [Ref 1].
- A vehicle is considered new if it has traveled less than 6,000 km or has been in use for less than six months [Ref 1].
- The right of disposal transferred to the buyer upon importation supports the assessment of VAT [Ref 1].
- Buyers should assess whether a vehicle qualifies as new or used to avoid unexpected VAT liabilities [Ref 1].
