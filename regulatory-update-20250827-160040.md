## Requirements / Specifications
- **Germany:** Structured XML formats are mandated for B2B e-invoicing [Ref 1]. Only the XML component of e-invoices needs to be archived [Ref 1].
- **Côte d’Ivoire:** E-invoices must include specific mandatory markers, such as a certification QR code and electronic fiscal seal [Ref 3].
- **EU (ViDA):** Structured e-invoices must follow a common EU format (e.g., EN16931) [Ref 20].
- **Romania:** Format: RO_CIUS (UBL/CII), aligned with EN 16931 [Ref 24].

## Validation / Business Rules
- **Germany:** Human-readable copies of e-invoices (e.g., PDFs) are required only if they contain additional tax-relevant information [Ref 1]. Incoming electronic documents must be retained in their original format [Ref 1].
- **NAV Online Invoice System:** Stricter validation rules will be implemented from September 2025, including new WARN messages, removal of some existing WARN messages, and conversion of some WARN messages to ERROR messages [Ref 8].
- **Romania:** Submission deadline: 5 calendar days from issuance [Ref 24]. Buyer retrieval: invoices available for 60 days [Ref 24].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **Germany:** VAT regulations have been updated to mandate structured XML formats for B2B e-invoicing [Ref 1]. The statutory invoice retention period has been reduced from 10 years to 8 years [Ref 1]. New credit note rules are effective from December 6, 2024 [Ref 1].
- **Nigeria:** The extension applies to companies with an annual turnover of ₦5 billion (approximately USD 3.1 million/EUR 2.7 million) [Ref 2].
- **EU (ViDA):** Mandatory E-Invoicing for intra-EU B2B and B2G transactions starting 1 July 2030 [Ref 20]. Real-time digital reporting of invoice data to national tax authorities [Ref 20].
- **Romania:** Since 1 January 2024, B2B e-invoicing is mandatory for Romanian-established businesses and for non-established VAT-registered taxpayers supplying goods or services with place of supply in Romania [Ref 24]. From 1 January 2025, the scope expanded to cover B2C transactions [Ref 24]. B2G invoicing has been mandatory since 2022 [Ref 24].
- **Pakistan:** The reform aims to enhance compliance, enable real-time sales tax reporting, and reduce VAT leakage by integrating business invoicing systems directly with the FBR [Ref 19].

## Submission & Interfaces
- **Côte d’Ivoire:** Companies must choose their issuance channels (API, web portal, mobile app, or electronic payment terminals) [Ref 3].
- **North Macedonia:** Companies can connect via APIs for integration with existing systems [Ref 16].
- **Romania:** Platform: ANAF SPV portal or APIs [Ref 24].

## Changes vs. Previous
- **Germany:** GoBD updated to reflect mandatory B2B e-invoicing [Ref 1].
- **Nigeria:** E-invoicing compliance deadline extended for large taxpayers [Ref 2].
- **Romania:** B2C invoices also routed via RO e-Factura; simplified invoices included [Ref 24].
- **Pakistan:** New deadlines for mandatory electronic invoicing starting September 2025 [Ref 19].

## Implementation Impact
- **Germany:** Businesses must comply with new e-invoicing regulations, including maintaining incoming electronic documents in their original formats and ensuring that any converted formats preserve the original structured data [Ref 1].
- **Côte d’Ivoire:** Companies must ensure compliance through proper data mapping, testing, and stakeholder training to avoid common pitfalls [Ref 3].
- **North Macedonia:** Businesses should prepare for the implementation timeline and adapt their invoicing systems [Ref 16].
- **Romania:** Companies need pre-validation, reject-handling loops, and fallback PDFs to avoid cash collection delays [Ref 24].
- **Pakistan:** Companies are advised to assess their categories based on turnover, upgrade their systems for FBR integration, utilize the testing phase to resolve issues, and train staff on new processes to ensure a smooth transition before the deadlines [Ref 19].
