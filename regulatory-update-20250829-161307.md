## Topic
- e-invoice updates norway.

## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction/scope: Norway. [Ref 1]

## Requirements / Specifications
- PEPPOL BIS Billing 3 is the recommended standard for e-invoicing in Norway [Ref 1].
- EHF Billing 3.0 (Elektronisk Handelsformat) is also mentioned as a previously used standard [Ref 2]. It is based on the PEPPOL BIS Billing 3.0 [Ref 2].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- According to the SAF-T regulations in Norway, all sales documents must be stored electronically and be available for potential audits [Ref 3].
- The SAF-T standard requires detailed specifications for financial data, including customer and supplier information, VAT details, and transaction data [Ref 3].

## Submission & Interfaces
- E-invoices in Norway are often exchanged through PEPPOL [Ref 1].

## Changes vs. Previous
- EHF Billing 3.0 was previously used, but PEPPOL BIS Billing 3 is now the recommended standard [Ref 1][Ref 2].

## Implementation Impact
- Businesses need to ensure their systems can generate and receive e-invoices in PEPPOL BIS Billing 3 format [Ref 1].
- Compliance with SAF-T regulations requires businesses to maintain electronic storage of sales documents and adhere to the SAF-T standard for financial data [Ref 3].

## Gaps/Unknowns
- Specific details about the transition from EHF Billing 3.0 to PEPPOL BIS Billing 3, including timelines or specific deprecation dates, are not provided in the given sources [Ref 1][Ref 2].
- Details on specific API endpoints or electronic submission channels for SAF-T data are not available in the provided sources [Ref 3].
