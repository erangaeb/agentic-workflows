## Requirements / Specifications (topic-specific)

### Germany [Ref 5]
- E-invoices must be in a structured XML format for B2B transactions starting January 1, 2025 [Ref 5].
- Only the XML component of e-invoices needs to be archived [Ref 5].
- Human-readable formats (e.g., PDFs) are required only if they contain additional tax-relevant information [Ref 5].
- Incoming electronic documents must be retained in their original format [Ref 5].

### Italy [Ref 12]
- E-invoices must be submitted via the Sistema di Interscambio (SDI) platform [Ref 12].
- The format must be FatturaPA XML, aligned with European Standard EN 16931 [Ref 12].

### Malaysia [Ref 8]
- E-invoices in currencies other than MYR must include a Currency Exchange Rate element from September 1, 2025 [Ref 8].
- Companies must update templates to include SST codes [Ref 8].

### Romania [Ref 16]
- Format: RO_CIUS (UBL/CII), aligned with EN 16931 [Ref 16].

### South Africa [Ref 6]
- E-Invoice is defined as a tax invoice in a structured electronic format for automatic processing [Ref 6].

## Validation / Business Rules (if relevant)

### Germany [Ref 5]
- The tax authority can request machine evaluations of the retained data, which must be provided in a machine-readable format [Ref 5].

### Italy [Ref 12]
- The SDI system performs format checks and supports national CIUS specifications [Ref 12].

### Romania [Ref 16]
- Companies need pre-validation and reject-handling loops [Ref 16].

## Tax & VAT Compliance (if relevant)

### Germany [Ref 5]
- Germany has updated its VAT regulations to mandate structured XML formats for B2B e-invoicing starting January 1, 2025 [Ref 5].

### Italy [Ref 12]
- Italy operates a Continuous Transaction Controls (CTC) model, with near real-time transmission of invoice data to the Revenue Agency [Ref 12].

### Malaysia [Ref 8]
- Starting July 1, 2025, Malaysia will revise its Sales and Service Tax (SST) system by expanding the taxable scope to include various services at an 8% rate [Ref 8].

### Pakistan [Ref 3]
- The reform aims to enhance compliance, enable real-time sales tax reporting, and reduce VAT leakage by integrating business invoicing systems directly with the FBR [Ref 3].

### Romania [Ref 16]
- Submission deadline: 5 calendar days from issuance [Ref 16].

## Submission & Interfaces (if relevant)

### Germany [Ref 5]
- The original format must be retained for audit purposes, allowing real-time access for tax authorities [Ref 5].

### Italy [Ref 12]
- All invoices must be submitted via the central Sistema di Interscambio (SDI) platform [Ref 12].

### Oman [Ref 15]
- Businesses must ensure their systems are integrated with the tax authority’s platform for real-time invoice submission [Ref 15].

### Romania [Ref 16]
- Platform: ANAF SPV portal or APIs [Ref 16].

## Changes vs. Previous (if the topic is an update)

### Germany [Ref 5]
- Germany has reduced the statutory invoice retention period from 10 years to 8 years [Ref 5].

## Implementation Impact

### Germany [Ref 5]
- Businesses must comply with new e-invoicing regulations starting January 1, 2025 [Ref 5].

### Pakistan [Ref 3]
- Companies are advised to assess their categories based on turnover, upgrade their systems for FBR integration, utilize the testing phase to resolve issues, and train staff on new processes to ensure a smooth transition before the deadlines [Ref 3].

### Romania [Ref 16]
- Companies need to reconcile VAT returns with RO e-Factura invoice statuses [Ref 16].
