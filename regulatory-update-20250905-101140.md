## Topic
- e-invoicing updates

## Document Metadata
- **UAE:** Implementation target date: 2026 [Ref 2]
- **North Macedonia:** Implementation target: Q3 2026 [Ref 3]
- **Poland:** Legislation signed on August 27, 2025 [Ref 4][Ref 8]

## Requirements / Specifications
- **North Macedonia:** e-Faktura reform includes standardized electronic invoices and digital signatures [Ref 3].
- **France:** Businesses must issue invoices in structured formats and receive them through certified platforms [Ref 5].

## Validation / Business Rules
- **Serbia:** If a taxpayer fails to submit a VAT return, the preliminary pre-filled return may be adopted as final by the tax authority [Ref 6].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **France:** Implementing a mandatory B2B electronic invoicing and e-reporting system to modernize VAT collection and combat fraud [Ref 5].
- **Serbia:** Introduces pre-filled VAT returns system effective January 2026, using electronic invoices data [Ref 6]. The pre-filled returns will be generated using data from the Sistem E-Faktura (SEF), Serbia’s electronic invoicing platform [Ref 6]. The POPDV summary form will be removed once the new system is in place [Ref 6].
- **North Macedonia:** Aims to improve VAT reporting and reduce tax evasion [Ref 3].

## Submission & Interfaces
- **North Macedonia:** Includes automation tools and API connectivity [Ref 3].
- **France:** The system uses the Portail Public de Facturation for invoice data flow [Ref 5].
- **Poland:** Full API documentation is available for developers and integrators [Ref 4].

## Changes vs. Previous
- **France:** Reduced Reporting Burden: B2C transactions and zero-value e-reporting are no longer required; no new mandatory data fields will be introduced [Ref 1]. Expanded Exemptions: Non-EU operations are exempt from e-reporting, and non-established entities get a deferral to 2027 for VAT self-assessment and intra-EU acquisitions [Ref 1]. SIREN Flexibility: No penalties for entities lacking a SIREN, plus a grace period for integration delays to avoid sanctions [Ref 1].
- **Poland:** KSeF 1.0 test environment was deactivated on September 1, 2025 [Ref 4].

## Implementation Impact
- **UAE:** Businesses operating in the UAE will need to adapt to these changes to ensure compliance with the upcoming mandatory e-invoicing regulations [Ref 2]. The transition to e-invoicing is expected to streamline operations and reduce the risk of errors [Ref 2].
- **North Macedonia:** Easing administrative burdens and enhancing financial control for businesses [Ref 3].
- **Poland:** KSeF 2.0 enhances data exchange and integration with third-party systems [Ref 4].
- **Poland:** Businesses that meet specific thresholds may continue issuing paper or electronic invoices outside of KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur) during transition periods [Ref 8]:
  - February 1 – March 31, 2026: Taxpayers whose total 2024 sales and VAT do not exceed PLN 200 million (approx. USD 54.6 million) may issue invoices in paper or standard electronic form [Ref 8].
  - April 1 – December 31, 2026: Taxpayers may use traditional invoicing if their monthly total sales and tax remain below PLN 10,000 (approx. USD 2,732) [Ref 8].
  - February 1 – December 31, 2026: Businesses may issue paper or electronic invoices linked to cash register receipts, provided they comply with fiscal cash register requirements [Ref 8].
