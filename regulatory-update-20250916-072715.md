## Topic
- e-invoice regulatory updates Poland.

## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction/scope: Poland. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- Publishing authority: Polish Ministry of Finance. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- Version/document date: The mandatory implementation of the National e-Invoicing System (KSeF) was initially planned for July 1, 2024, for most taxpayers and January 1, 2025, for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) with sales below PLN 200 million. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- Effective/compliance dates (incl. grace periods):
    - The mandatory implementation of KSeF has been postponed indefinitely. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
    - The new Minister of Finance, Andrzej Domański, announced the postponement on January 19, 2024. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
    - The decision followed an audit that revealed critical errors in the KSeF system's architecture and performance, making it unsuitable for mandatory implementation on the planned dates. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
    - The Ministry of Finance plans to present a new implementation date and a new legislative process for KSeF in April or May 2024. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
    - Voluntary use of KSeF remains possible. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]

## Requirements / Specifications
- The KSeF system is designed for structured electronic invoices. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- Invoices are to be issued and received through the KSeF platform. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- The format for e-invoices in KSeF is a specific XML structure (FA_VAT). [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- The scope of mandatory e-invoicing was intended to cover B2B transactions. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- Invoices issued to consumers (B2C) and simplified invoices were initially excluded from mandatory KSeF. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- The Ministry of Finance is considering expanding the scope to include B2C invoices and simplified invoices in the future. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]

## Validation / Business Rules
- The audit identified critical errors in the KSeF system, including performance issues and architectural flaws. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- The system was found to be unable to handle the expected volume of invoices, particularly during peak periods. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- The audit also revealed issues with system stability and security. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- KSeF is Poland's Continuous Transaction Control (CTC) system for VAT purposes. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- The system aims to enhance tax collection and combat VAT fraud. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- Once mandatory, invoices issued outside KSeF would not be considered valid. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- Penalties for non-compliance were planned to be introduced from January 1, 2025, for most taxpayers, and July 1, 2025, for SMEs. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7] These penalty dates are now also postponed. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]

## Submission & Interfaces
- Taxpayers can access KSeF via a dedicated web application or integrate their accounting systems using an API. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- Authentication methods include qualified electronic signature, trusted profile (ePUAP), or KSeF authorization token. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- The system assigns a unique KSeF identification number to each invoice upon successful submission. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]

## Changes vs. Previous
- **Postponement of Mandatory Implementation:** The most significant change is the indefinite postponement of the mandatory KSeF implementation, which was originally set for July 1, 2024, for most taxpayers and January 1, 2025, for SMEs. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- **Reason for Postponement:** The decision was made due to critical errors and performance issues identified in the KSeF system during an audit. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- **New Timeline:** The Ministry of Finance will announce new implementation dates and a revised legislative process in April or May 2024. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- **Voluntary Use:** Voluntary use of KSeF remains available. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- **Scope Review:** The Ministry is considering expanding the scope of KSeF to include B2C invoices and simplified invoices, which were previously excluded. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- **Penalty Dates:** The introduction of penalties for non-compliance is also postponed in line with the mandatory implementation. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]

## Implementation Impact
- **For Businesses:**
    - **Relief from immediate compliance pressure:** Businesses that were preparing for the July 1, 2024, deadline now have an extended period to adapt. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
    - **Uncertainty regarding new deadlines:** The indefinite postponement creates uncertainty for planning and budgeting for KSeF integration. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
    - **Potential for revised requirements:** The Ministry's review might lead to changes in technical specifications or scope, requiring further adjustments. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
    - **Continued voluntary use:** Companies that have already integrated or wish to benefit from KSeF can continue to use it voluntarily. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- **For the Ministry of Finance:**
    - **System overhaul:** The Ministry must address the critical errors and performance issues identified in the audit. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
    - **Rebuilding trust:** The postponement and the reasons behind it may impact public and business trust in the system's readiness. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
    - **Legislative process:** A new legislative process will be required to establish the revised mandatory dates. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]

## Gaps/Unknowns
- The exact new mandatory implementation dates for KSeF are unknown, pending an announcement in April or May 2024. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- The specific details of the "critical errors" and "architectural flaws" in the KSeF system have not been fully disclosed to the public. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- Whether the technical specifications (e.g., XML schema) will undergo significant changes as part of the system overhaul is not yet clear. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
- The precise timeline and legislative steps for expanding KSeF's scope to B2C invoices and simplified invoices are not defined. [Ref 1][Ref 2][Ref 3][Ref 4][Ref 5][Ref 6][Ref 7]
