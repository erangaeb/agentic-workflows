## Document Metadata
- Jurisdiction/scope: Vietnam [Ref 1]
- Publishing authority: Official Gazette [Ref 1]
- Version/document date: Decree No. 181/2025/ND-CP [Ref 1]
- Effective/compliance dates: July 1 [Ref 1]

## Requirements / Specifications
- **VAT rate structure adjustments:**
  - A zero percent VAT rate is applied to exported goods and services [Ref 1].
  - A 5 percent VAT rate applies to specified goods [Ref 1].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- **Exemptions:**
  - A list of nontaxable goods and services is clarified, covering specified agricultural products, transfers of land use rights, and certain financial, banking, and securities services [Ref 1].
- **Taxable price definitions:**
  - Specified for asset leasing, goods processing, and construction and installation activities [Ref 1].
- **Eligibility for deduction method:**
  - Businesses with annual sales of 1 billion Vietnamese dong (approx. US$38,032) or more may apply the deduction method, subject to certain exceptions [Ref 1].
- **Refund mechanism enhancements:**
  - Monthly or quarterly input VAT refunds are available for businesses with unrefunded input VAT totaling 300 million dong (approx. US$11,411) or more [Ref 1].
  - Investment projects initiated before July 1 are eligible for VAT refunds, including those still in the investment phase as of that date [Ref 1].
