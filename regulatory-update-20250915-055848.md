## Topic
- Sweden e-invoicing updates.

## Requirements / Specifications
- Swedish government agencies are required to receive e-invoices according to the European standard EN 16931 [Ref 1].
- The Swedish standard Svefaktura is still supported, but EN 16931 is preferred [Ref 1].

## Tax & VAT Compliance
- E-invoicing in Sweden helps to combat VAT fraud [Ref 2].
- E-invoicing contributes to fair competition and reduces administrative burdens [Ref 2].

## Submission & Interfaces
- PEPPOL is the most common network for sending e-invoices to Swedish government agencies [Ref 1].

## Implementation Impact
- Suppliers to Swedish government agencies need to be able to send e-invoices that comply with EN 16931 [Ref 1].
- Businesses should check with their customers which format is preferred [Ref 1].
